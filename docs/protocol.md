# Evaluation protocol

All results are simulated on a synthetic market with a planted, known signal. They show whether the formulations and the simulator behave as designed and how the strategies compare in this simulation, under these assumptions; they say nothing about real markets or profitability.

## One declared criterion

`ce_ann = ppy * (mean(r) - (gamma_ce / 2) * var(r))`: the annualized certainty equivalent of the net per-bar returns after the first decision (`var` with ddof 1, `gamma_ce = 6`, `ppy = 252`). It is the only quantity that selects a strategy's configuration in the tuner, orders a table of strategies, or decides a highlight (`experiments/criterion.py`; a static test checks that the tuner and the table builder rank by nothing else). Every other metric (net and gross Sharpe ratio, annualized return and volatility, maximum drawdown, historical CVaR 95 percent, turnover, cost drag, exposures, the share of held decisions) is reported, never used to select or rank. S4 (risk models) and S7 (execution policies) compare components, not strategies, and have their own declared quantities: the realized out-of-sample volatility of the minimum-variance portfolio (S4), and the mean implementation shortfall with its spread across paths and `E + lambda V` on the frontier (S7).

## Equal effort, frozen configurations, disjoint seeds

- **Registry.** `experiments/configs/registry.json` holds the strategies (forecast provider, risk model, optimizer, book), their search spaces (at most four parameters each), the trial counts, the seeds, and every experiment grid. The tuner, the freeze, and the runner read it.
- **Tuning.** Every strategy with parameters is tuned by random search on the `base` market with the tuning seeds 100 to 103 and the same number of trials (16): trial `i` of a strategy is drawn from the stream `tune.<strategy>` with seed `i`, so it is the same configuration whatever else is tuned. The best trial has the highest mean `ce_ann` over the tuning seeds; ties go to the lowest trial index. `EW` has no parameters; `RANK_LS` gets a one-parameter search with the same 16 trials; `MV_NC` is tuned like `MV` with `cost_scale` fixed at 0. The tuner refuses any seed outside 100 to 199. The log (`experiments/tuned/tuning_log.csv`) and the trial registry (`trials.csv`) are committed.
- **Bounds with their own budgets.** The information ladder of S2b re-tunes only `gamma` of MV on a grid of 8 values per rung (same grid and seeds for every rung). The `RETUNED` bound of S5 re-tunes every strategy with the same 16 trials on shift markets of the tuning seeds, scored on the bars from the shift on. Both are labelled as bounds wherever they appear.
- **Freeze.** After the tuning run, `experiments/tuned/frozen.json` lists the configuration hash of everything the full evaluation runs (generated from the registry and the tuned configurations). The evaluation refuses a seed below 1000 and a configuration whose hash is not in that file. A change to result-affecting code (everything under `src/` except the report and table builders, plus `configs/` and `experiments/configs/`) after the freeze means tuning and the evaluation run again.
- **Seeds.** Development and tests 1 to 99; tuning 100 to 199 (the protocol uses 100 to 103); evaluation 1000 and up (S2, S5, SENS: 1000 to 1029; S3, S4, S6: 1000 to 1019; S6(b): 1000; S7: 1000 to 1019 with 2000 paths each; S8: 1000 to 1009). The Tier-2 studies use the seeds of the Tier-1 experiment they extend (`WDRO`, `GP_AIM`, fat tails: those of S2 and S5; cost misspecification and capacity: those of S3; adaptive execution: those of S7). A seed fixes the whole market, so every strategy of a comparison sees the same data (common random numbers).
- **Quick mode.** `--quick` runs every experiment on the `tiny` market (and `tiny_shift` for S5), on the development seeds 1 to 3 with the tuning seeds 100 and 101 and 4 trials, with its own frozen file `experiments/tuned/frozen_quick.json`; every output is labelled `QUICK` and the table builder refuses it.

## The cash reference and flat strategies

`CASH` (no positions, `ce_ann` exactly 0) is a reference row of S2 next to `EW` and `RANK_LS`; it is not one of the eight registered strategies (not a trial of the deflated Sharpe ratio). Because a strategy that holds nothing scores exactly 0, the share of bars after the first decision without any position (`flat_frac`) is reported next to every strategy. The search spaces of `ROBUST` (`kappa_rob` in [0, 1]) and `CVAR` (`eta_cvar` in [0.0003, 0.03]) were narrowed before the tuning run (review 1): with the plain forecast the box penalty is proportional to `|mu|`, so `kappa_rob` above the forecast's t-statistic holds nothing, and CVaR is positively homogeneous, so `eta_cvar` above a threshold holds nothing; the wider ranges gave these two strategies an all-cash option that the others cannot reach.

## No strategy has information or effort another lacks

The same market, seeds, costs, warmup, schedule, and `plain` forecast for every strategy of a comparison; the same tuning budget and seeds. A strategy with an oracle forecast is a bound and carries `ORACLE` in every log, manifest, and table (the table builder refuses an unmarked oracle row); a configuration re-tuned on data from after a shift carries `RETUNED`.

## Statistics

The independent unit is the seed. Means come with 95 percent Student-t intervals across seeds; comparisons are paired differences (strategy minus reference on the same seed) with intervals and win, tie, and loss counts (an exact tie is a tie); proportions get Wilson intervals; every Monte Carlo number has its standard error next to it. In S2 the deflated Sharpe ratio is computed per seed for the strategy with the highest per-bar Sharpe ratio, with the eight registered strategies of the table as the trials (`N = 8`, `V` the variance of their per-bar Sharpe ratios on that seed); the share of seeds in which it exceeds 0.95 is reported next to the paired intervals. This departs from the contract, where every evaluated configuration is a trial: the tuning trials ran on disjoint tuning seeds. The DSR assumes selection by the Sharpe ratio while the declared criterion is `ce_ann`. The comparisons of S2b, S3, S5, S6, and S7 are paired differences between variants on common random numbers, not selections among trials, so the DSR does not apply to them.

## What the README highlights

Fixed before the evaluation: among the non-oracle strategies of S2, the one with the highest mean `ce_ann` is named; it is called better than a reference (`CASH`, `EW`, `RANK_LS`) only if the 95 percent paired interval of its `ce_ann` against that reference excludes zero (against `CASH`: its own interval); otherwise the README says that no strategy separated from that reference. Exact ties are called ties. Results that look bad for the project (a robust variant that loses, a controller that does not close the gap, an optimizer beaten by equal weight) are reported as they came out.

## Order of work

Code complete, protocol frozen (registry), review 1, fixes, the protocol tuning run once, the tuned configurations and `frozen.json` committed, the full evaluation once on the final result-affecting code, then figures and documentation, review 2, and documentation fixes only.

## Tier 2

Every Tier-2 strategy (`WDRO`, `GP_AIM`; `experiments/configs/registry_tier2.json`) is tuned once with the protocol of S2 (16 trials, tuning seeds 100-103, stream `tune.<strategy>`, best mean `ce_ann`) and frozen in `experiments/tuned/frozen_tier2.json`, together with every loop configuration of the Tier-2 studies (fat tails, cost misspecification, capacity), before they run; the Tier-1 files stay untouched. The Tier-2 evaluation refuses a configuration that is in neither frozen file and applies the seed rules unchanged. Tier-2 code may add modules and options but must not change any Tier-1 result: the SHA-256 of every quick result file (without `wall_` columns) recorded at the evaluation commit (`experiments/results/quick_sha256.txt`) is reproduced after each Tier-2 change, on the build machine (bit-for-bit equality holds on one platform and software stack only; on Linux with the same package versions the quick results agree to about 1e-3 relative per run but not byte for byte). Tier-2 results are in `experiments/results/tier2/`.
